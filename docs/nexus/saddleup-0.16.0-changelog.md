# GK2 Saddle Up 0.16.0

Includes GK2 Donkey API 0.44.0. Built for game 1.011.1.

- Added an option to leave your mounts in the world: when you call another mount, the one you had stays where it is with its babies and is kept with your save. Pick it again in the Mount Journal and it comes back to you. Off by default (Options, Mount). "Send your other mounts home" sends them all home.
- A mount you call from far away now brings its babies with it.
- The options have five tabs now: Mount Behaviour and Sound are part of the Mount tab.
- Every row in the colours window names the part it colours, and skins can have rows for parts of their own (the Donkey Skin Pack 1.2.0 has them for all its skins).
- Added colour rows for the donkey's outline, its legs and mane, and its eyes. Game default keeps them as the game draws them.
- Babies take their own shade of every part you colour.
- Fixed a coat colour on Hellfire coming out darker than picked, and a few of the donkey's pixels under a skin taking the harness colour.
- Fixed zombies on the cart showing as an icon during Halloween.
- Fixed a jump over things sometimes putting you back where you jumped from.
- Translation fixes in Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Korean.
- Your settings and saves carry over. A colour you set for a part that now has its own row stays on that part.
