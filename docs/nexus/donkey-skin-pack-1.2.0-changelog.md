# GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skin Pack 1.2.0

- Added Armored, a new look for your donkey: plate armour with an ornate knight's helmet and a plume that sways as it walks.
- Each skin's parts have colour rows of their own (needs GK2 Saddle Up 0.16.0): Royal Parade's saddle cloth, crown, trim and fringe, emblem and jewels; Armored's helmet, plume, body armour, skirt and brass; Hellfire's horns, glowing eyes, lava cracks, tail and tail tip.
- Hellfire's horns and Armored's steel lines follow the new Outline row.
- Added localization support: French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean.
- Polished Hellfire and Royal Parade: Hellfire's horns and ears look right when the donkey turns and its eyes glow while it grazes; Royal Parade's crown stays on the head through the turn and the blink.
- The pack is now named "GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skin Pack" in the log and in mod lists.
- Your skins' colours carry over: a part that used to follow the harness or the second tone keeps that colour.
