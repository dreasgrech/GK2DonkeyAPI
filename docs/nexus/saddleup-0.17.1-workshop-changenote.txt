GK2 Saddle Up 0.17.1

- Works with game 1.012 (the update of 13 November): an earlier version of the mod turns itself off on it, with a note in the log.
- New colours window: one palette for every part, named colours in your language, tabs for the mount, the skin and the cart.
- Ride another mount without getting off first; "Load on cart" only when you face the cart.
- The Mount Journal and its windows pause the game like the game's own, with the game's window and button sounds.
- H and G work in the Mount Journal; the skull is renamed from Gerry to Larry; the keeper sits still on a flying mount.
- Smoother on slower PCs, and fixes for skins' edges, the cart's picture, the pause menu, babies and the cart key next to walls. Your settings and saves carry over.
