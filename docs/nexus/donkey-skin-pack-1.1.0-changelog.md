# GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skin Pack 1.1.0

- **Armored**, a third look for your donkey: plate barding with a plumed chanfron, a chest plate and riveted flank
  plates over red padding. The steel follows the second tone you pick (black iron, bronze or gold), the plume and the
  padding your harness colour.
- The skins' names and descriptions in the game's language: French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean (with GK2 Saddle Up 0.15.0).
- The pack's name in the log and in mod lists is now "GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skin Pack".
- Your donkey's skin and colours carry over.
