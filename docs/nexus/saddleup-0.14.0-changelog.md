# GK2 Saddle Up 0.14.0: Saddle Up's own options window

For players. GK2 Donkey API 0.42.0 is in the download. Built for game 1.011 (Steam build 26297960).

- **Saddle Up's own options window.** The game's Settings window (in the pause menu and the main menu) has a new
  **GK2 Saddle Up options** button, and the Mounts panel (L) an **Options** button. If you have GK2 Mod Framework
  installed, Saddle Up's Settings button in its Mods menu opens it too.
- The sections are on the left and their options on the right; the line at the bottom says what the option under
  the mouse does. **Defaults** sets the section shown back to its defaults, after asking.
- **Keys are set in the window:** click a key's box and press the key, with Shift, Ctrl or Alt, or one of those on its
  own (Climb's Left Shift and Descend's Left Ctrl can be changed now). Backspace: no key. Esc: cancel. A key another
  option, another mod or the game already uses is said under the option.
- **Two keys on one key:** of two of your keys on the same key, the more specific acts alone. With Send your mount away
  on Ctrl + G, Ctrl + G no longer drives the cart too.
- **Typing in another text box** (another mod's, or the game's search box on the Tech Tree) no longer presses Saddle
  Up's keys. Typing an L there opened the Mounts panel.
- **The Mounts panel over the Character window**, the inventory or a chest: that window waits underneath and comes
  back with Escape; Ride and Drive the cart close it. The panel's buttons line up with the list and the picture, and
  its Done button is gone (its X and Escape close it).
- The "Showing" pages in the game's Settings window are gone. Your settings and saves carry over unchanged. The Dragons
  Mount Pack and the Donkey Skin Pack need no update.
