# GK2 Saddle Up 0.15.0: in your language

For players. GK2 Donkey API 0.43.0 is in the download. Built for game 1.011 (Steam build 26297960).

- **Saddle Up speaks the game's languages:** French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean. With the game in one of them, the options window, the Mount
  Journal and its colours window, the keeper's thought bubbles, the prompts over your mount and the crossroads
  notification follow it.
- **Translate it into another language yourself:** each mod has its English as a spreadsheet file,
  `Languages\en.csv`, next to its DLL. Copy it, translate the value column and save it as "CSV UTF-8" under your
  language's name (`de.csv`, `fr.csv`, ...). The README's Translations section says how; the log names any line it
  couldn't read.
- **A few messages are worded differently** so that every language can say them: the cart's cargo is a number ("4",
  or "2 of 4"), and an overloaded cart says "Too much on the cart (6 of at most 4). Take some off first."
- The key that opens the Mount Journal is called Mount Journal in the options.
- Your settings and saves carry over unchanged. Update the Dragons Mount Pack and the Donkey Skin Pack to see their
  mounts and skins in your language too.
