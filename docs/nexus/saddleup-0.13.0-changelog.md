# GK2 Saddle Up 0.13.0: what's new since 0.12.0

For players. GK2 Donkey API 0.41.0 is in the download. Everything you set in 0.12.0 carries over; the packs (Dragon,
Critters, Donkey Skins 1.0.0) stay as they are.

## Colours per skin

- **Everything in the Colours window is kept for the skin you are customizing**, not for the mount: coat, harness,
  second tone, the cart's paint, wheels, hubs, the lantern's light, and whether the lantern shows and stays lit by day.
  A Hellfire donkey keeps its reds, the plain donkey its brown, and switching skins in the Mount Journal brings each
  one's colours back.
- The window's title names the skin ("Colours: Donkey - Hellfire").
- **What you had set in 0.12.0 goes to the skin you were wearing.** The mount's other skins start at the game's
  defaults until you customize them. The name, the babies and the volumes stay per mount, as before.

## Fixes

- The Mount Journal's picture is centred on what it shows: a skin whose lantern is off (or where the lamp isn't
  allowed) sat low and left of the middle, in a frame kept from when the lamp was there.
- The journal's picture shows the lantern setting of the skin you are looking at, not of the one you wear, and
  follows the toggle at once.
