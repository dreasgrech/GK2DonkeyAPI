[h1]GK2 Saddle Up - Donkey Skin Pack[/h1]
A donkey skin pack for the donkey mount in the Saddle Up mod
[b]Hellfire[/b], [b]Royal Parade[/b] and [b]Armored[/b]: three new looks for your donkey mount.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Skins[/h2]

[h3]Hellfire[/h3]
Horns, a devil's tail, glowing eyes and lava cracks over an ash coat. The bishop does not approve. The horns, the glowing eyes, the lava cracks, the tail and its tip each take a colour of your own in the colours window.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/hellfire-riding.png?v=2[/img]

[h3]Royal Parade[/h3]
A crown and a royal saddle cloth, fit for a coronation or at least a good funeral. The saddle cloth, the crown, the trim and fringe, the emblem and the jewels each take a colour of your own; on Game default they stay as drawn, the crown gold.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/royal-parade-riding.png?v=2[/img]

[h3]Armored[/h3]
Plate barding for a war donkey: an ornate knight's helmet with a plume that sways as it walks, a chest plate, steel plates over the body and a cloth skirt under them. The helmet, the plume, the body armour, the skirt and the brass each take a colour of your own.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/armored-riding.png?v=1[/img]

[h3]Pick it in the Mount Journal[/h3]
Press [b]L[/b], pick the Donkey, and choose the skin in its [b]Skin[/b] list. The picture shows it with the cart before you ride. Your coat, harness and cart colours still apply under the skin, and the choice is kept with your save.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/journal-hellfire.png?v=2[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/journal-royal-parade.png?v=2[/img]

[h3]Customize it[/h3]
The Colours window names each skin's own parts next to the donkey's own rows, and the cart's paint, wheels and hubs, with the preview of your real donkey. Colours you set before carry over: a part that followed the harness or the second tone keeps that colour.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/colours-hellfire.png?v=3[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/colours-royal-parade.png?v=3[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-skin-pack/colours-armored.png?v=1[/img]

[h3]The babies wear it too[/h3]
Your donkey's babies look like their parent: little horns and cracks, little crowns and cloths, or little helmets, each its own size and shade.

[h3]Languages[/h3]
Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean (with GK2 Saddle Up 0.15.0 or newer).

[h2]Install[/h2]

[b]A BepInEx plugin.[/b] The game ships BepInEx and installs the plugin itself; nothing to copy.

Needs [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3830136851]GK2 Saddle Up[/url] (it brings GK2 Donkey API with it): Steam lists it as this item's required item, so subscribe to both.

[olist]
[*]Subscribe. The game installs the pack itself: on the next start the main menu's [b]Mods[/b] window lists it with its version and an Enable/Disable switch.
[*]Start the game, press [b]L[/b], pick the Donkey: Hellfire, Royal Parade and Armored are in its Skin list.
[/olist]
[b]Steam Deck and Linux (Proton).[/b] Set the game's launch options to [code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%[/code] so BepInEx loads.

[b]Installed from Nexus Mods before?[/b] Delete the folder [b]GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack[/b] from [i]BepInEx\plugins[/i] in the game folder before you subscribe; two copies of one plugin make BepInEx skip one with a warning.

[b]Update.[/b] Steam updates the item and the game copies it in on the next start. The pack is one file with everything inside, so nothing is left over.

[b]Unsubscribe.[/b] The game removes the pack's files on the next start. A donkey that wore one of the skins shows as the game's own donkey again; the Skin list says "Hellfire (not installed)", and the moment the pack is back it wears it again. Your save is never touched.

[b]Found a bug?[/b] Post it in the Discussions here and attach [i]BepInEx\LogOutput.log[/i] from the game folder (it is rewritten each time the game starts, so grab it right after the problem).

[h2]The Saddle Up mod[/h2]

This pack needs the GK2 Saddle Up mod, which gives you a donkey mount: call it, ride it, drive the cart, haul logs, stone and corpses on it, leave it standing with its load and find it where you left it.
