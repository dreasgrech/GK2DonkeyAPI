[center][size=6][b][color=#f0b45a]GK2 Saddle Up - Flying Dragons Mount Pack[/color][/b][/size]
[size=4]A dragon mount pack for the Saddle Up mod[/size]
[size=3][b]The Dragon[/b]: a mount that flies. No fence, river or roof in the village can stop it.[/size]
[/center]
[line]
[size=5][b][color=#f0b45a]The mount[/color][/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Dragon[/b][/size]
[size=3]Nobody knows where it came from, and it isn't saying. Scales, a spiked tail and a big belly, twice the donkey's size, with a roar, wing beats and a landing thud of its own. It is slow on its feet and it won't pull a cart. It flies instead.[/size]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/dragons-mount-pack/dragon-keeper.png?v=2[/img][/center]

[size=4][b]Fly[/b][/size]
[size=3]Press [b]Space[/b] to jump, and Space again in the air to take off. Hold [b]Left Shift[/b] to climb and [b]Left Ctrl[/b] to descend; Space while flying brings you down. Fly over the river, the cliffs, the fences and the roofs. Fly low and you bump into houses and trees; fly high and you pass over them. It lands wherever you can stand, so over water it holds its height and tells you. All four keys can be changed in Saddle Up's settings.[/size]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/dragons-mount-pack/dragon-flying.gif?v=2[/img][/center]
[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/dragons-mount-pack/dragon-flying.png?v=2[/img][/center]

[size=3]No flying indoors. In your armour Space attacks, as in the game, so give Jump and Fly another key if you want to take off during a fight. Saving in the air lands you first, and so does getting off.[/size]

[size=4][b]Pick it in the Mount Journal[/b][/size]
[size=3]Press [b]L[/b], pick the Dragon, and press [b]H[/b] to ride it. Its page says what it is: Slow, no cart, babies, and it flies. [b]G[/b] gets you "A dragon can't pull the cart"; a cart you were driving stays where you left it and waits for your next mount that pulls one.[/size]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/dragons-mount-pack/journal-dragon.png?v=2[/img][/center]

[size=4][b]Customize it[/b][/size]
[size=3]The Colours window works on the dragon as on the donkey: the coat colour for its scales and the second tone for its belly, presets or a custom colour, with the preview of your real dragon. It has no harness and no cart, so those rows don't apply to it. Its name, its babies and its voice volume are in the journal.[/size]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/dragons-mount-pack/colours-dragon.png?v=2[/img][/center]

[size=4][b]The babies[/b][/size]
[size=3]Up to five little dragons follow yours around, jump when it jumps and answer its roar ([b]V[/b]) with growls of their own. They can follow you on foot and come indoors with you, like the donkey's.[/size]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/dragons-mount-pack/babies-dragon.png?v=2[/img][/center]

[line]
[size=5][b][color=#f0b45a]Install[/color][/b][/size]

[size=3]Needs [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/391]GK2 Saddle Up[/url] 0.12.0 or newer (it brings GK2 Donkey API 0.40.0 with it) and BepInEx 5, installed as on the Saddle Up page.[/size]

[list=1]
[*][size=3]Extract the download into the game folder (where [i]GraveyardKeeper2.exe[/i] is). It adds the folder [b]GK2SaddleUpDragonsMountPack[/b] to [i]BepInEx\plugins[/i]. The game's own [b]Mods[/b] window (main menu) lists only the mods the game installed itself from the Steam Workshop: installed from this download, the mod works fully but is not listed there. That is normal.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3]Start the game, press [b]L[/b]: the Dragon is in the mount list. Pick it and press [b]H[/b].[/size][/*]
[/list]

[size=3][b]Update.[/b] Replace the folder. The pack is one file with everything inside, so nothing is left over.

[b]Remove.[/b] Delete the folder [b]GK2SaddleUpDragonsMountPack[/b] from [i]BepInEx\plugins[/i]. A save that rode the dragon shows "Dragon (not installed)" in the journal and the donkey comes when you press H; the moment the pack is back you ride the dragon again, with its name, colours and babies as you left them. Your save is never touched.

[b]Found a bug?[/b] Report it in this page's Bugs tab and attach [i]BepInEx\LogOutput.log[/i] from the game folder (it is rewritten each time the game starts, so grab it right after the problem).[/size]
