[h1]GK2 Saddle Up[/h1]
Your own donkey and cart in Graveyard Keeper 2.

Call it, ride it, drive the cart, haul logs, stone and corpses on it, leave it standing with its load and find it where you left it.

Available in 11 languages.
[hr][/hr]
[h2]What you get[/h2]

[h3]Your own donkey as a mount[/h3]
Press [b]H[/b] anywhere and it comes to you; you ride.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-comes.gif?v=2[/img]

Press [b]G[/b] and it comes with its cart; you drive.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/riding-cart.gif?v=2[/img]

Twice your walking speed on the donkey, one and a half times with the cart.

[h3]Name it, and give it foals[/h3]
"[E] Ride Balthazar". Up to five baby donkeys follow yours around, and can come indoors with you.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/Foals.png?v=2[/img]

[h3]A cart that carries six[/h3]
Everything you pick up while driving goes on the cart, stacked in three layers. Piles, tables, graves and quest hand-ins take from it as if you held the item. Each mount pulls its own load: the donkey six, Larry four.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/cargo-sixitems-cycle.gif?v=2[/img]

[h3]Haul corpses[/h3]
Bodies ride on the cart like anything else. Drive to the morgue door, step off, and you walk in with the body in your hands.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/corpse-to-morgue.gif?v=2[/img]

[h3]Zombies ride too[/h3]
Pick up a zombie while driving and it lies on the cart, ready to hand to its work.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/ZombieWorkstation.png?v=2[/img]

[h3]A storage chest on wheels[/h3]
Drive up to a storage chest and take it with you, everything inside. Set it down wherever you want it.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/chest-on-cart.gif?v=2[/img]

[h3]Fight from the saddle[/h3]
In your armour you swing the sword and shoot the bow from the saddle, aim up and down, and the donkey backs away with you while you aim. The cart is left standing when your armour goes on.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/fight-a.gif?v=2[/img]

[h3]Ride Larry![/h3]
Larry comes with the mod: the talking skull, three times his usual size, hopping along on pure opinion and sharing it in his own voice. Fast, with a cart that holds four, and little skulls trailing him. Press [b]L[/b] for the Mount Journal, pick Larry and ride.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/gerry-riding.gif?v=2[/img]

[h3]Your donkey, your colours[/h3]
Every part by its name: the donkey's coat, muzzle, legs, mane and ears, eyes, outline and harness, and a skin's own parts. The cart's paint, wheels and hubs, the lantern's light. 24 named colours or Original, with a live preview. Babies wear each in their own shade.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreasgrech/GK2DonkeyAPI/main/media/donkey-cart-colors-cycle.gif?v=2[/img]

[h3]Leave it standing[/h3]
Get off anywhere and your donkey, cart and load stay there. Load and unload a standing cart on foot with [b]E[/b].
Kept with your save, the load still on the cart, and Steam Cloud carries it along.

[h3]Leave your mounts in the world[/h3]
An option (Options, Mount): call another mount and the one you had stays where it is with its babies, kept with your save; pick it again in the Mount Journal and it comes back. [b]Send your other mounts home[/b] sends them all home.

[h3]Optional: bodies left at the crossroads[/h3]
Off by default. The game's delivery donkey drops its corpses by the nun's crossroads, and you cart them in yourself.

[h2]Keys[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]H[/b] — your donkey comes and you ride. Riding: get off. Driving: leave the cart standing and ride on.
[*][b]G[/b] — your donkey comes with its cart and you drive. Riding: the cart comes and is hitched. Driving: get off.
[*][b]E[/b] — at your donkey: get on. At the cart: load what you hold, or take the top item with empty hands. Driving with nothing in front: set the top item down behind the cart (a chest too).
[*][b]L[/b] — the Mount Journal: pick your mount (the donkey, Larry, or a pack's), name it, choose its skin and babies, open its colours.
[*][b]B[/b] — ring the bell.
[*][b]V[/b] — your mount calls out, and its babies answer.
[*][b]Space[/b] — your mount jumps. On a mount that flies, Space again in the air takes off.
[*][b]Send your mount away[/b] (no key until you set one) — your mount, its cart and its babies go; the load is set down first. H or G calls one again.
[/list]
All keys can be changed. You get off by yourself for work, ladders, fishing, sleep and at building doors.

[h2]Settings[/h2]

The game's [b]Settings[/b] window (pause menu or main menu) has a [b]GK2 Saddle Up options[/b] button. The Mount Journal (L) has an [b]Options[/b] button too. Five tabs: Keys, Mount, Cart, Safe spot and Freeplay (one speed and the most cargo for every mount). Everything is also in [i]BepInEx\config\dreas.gk2.saddleup.cfg[/i], to edit with the game closed.

[h2]Install[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Subscribe. The game installs the mod itself: on the next start the main menu's [b]Mods[/b] window lists GK2 Saddle Up. Nothing to copy, no BepInEx to install (the game ships it since 1.011).
[*]Start the game and load a save. Press [b]H[/b] to call your donkey and [b]G[/b] to call the cart.
[/olist]
[b]Steam Deck and Linux (Proton).[/b] Set the game's launch options to [code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%[/code] so BepInEx loads.

[b]Installed from Nexus Mods before?[/b] Delete the folders [b]GK2DonkeyAPI[/b] and [b]GK2SaddleUp[/b] from [i]BepInEx\plugins[/i] before you subscribe: the game must own one copy. Your settings and saves stay.

[b]Safe for your save.[/b] Nothing in your save is ever touched: it loads fine without the mod, and the donkey waits in its own small file next to it.

[b]Game updates.[/b] Built for game 1.012.7. After a game update the mod checks the game's code at start: a part the update changed stays off until this item is updated, and the log says so.

[b]Unsubscribe.[/b] Unload the cart and set any chest down first. The game removes the mod's files on the next start.

[b]Found a bug?[/b] Post it in the Discussions here and attach [i]BepInEx\LogOutput.log[/i] from the game folder right after the problem (each start rewrites it).

[h2]For modders[/h2]

The mod is two parts: the [b]GK2 Donkey API[/b], which gives mod authors full control of the mounts, the cart, the cargo and the looks, and [b]Saddle Up[/b], a playable showcase of it. This item contains the API; a mod that builds on it lists this item as its required item. The source code is coming with it.
