# GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skins

A skins pack for GK2 Saddle Up: two new looks for the donkey.

- **Hellfire:** horns, a devil's tail, glowing eyes and lava cracks over an ash coat. The cracks follow your second
  tone colour.
- **Royal Parade:** a crown and a royal saddle cloth. The cloth follows your harness colour.

- **Needs:** GK2 Donkey API 0.40.0 or newer and GK2 Saddle Up (it offers the skins).
- **Install:** put the folder `GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkins` into `BepInEx\plugins\` (the zip has it so):
  `BepInEx\plugins\GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkins\GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkins.dll`, a plugin with the pack's files inside. To
  update, replace it.
- **In the game:** in the Mounts panel (L), pick the Donkey; its Skin list has Hellfire and Royal Parade.
- **Remove:** delete the folder `GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkins`. A donkey that wore one of them shows the game's donkey, and
  your choice comes back when the pack does.

The skins: `donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.hellfire` and `donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.royal-parade` (skins for the
game's donkey: a folder `donkey\skins\` with no `mount.cfg`), and Hellfire's effect `saddleup-donkey-skins.hellfire`.
Its files in the repo: `packs/SaddleUpDonkeySkins/gk2mounts/`; the pack format: docs/skins.md and
docs/making-a-mount.md.
