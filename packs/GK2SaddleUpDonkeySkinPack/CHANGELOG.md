# GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skins, changes

## Unreleased

- **Royal Parade's saddle cloth sits on the back while walking** (2026-11-11): in three walk frames the donkey's coat
  showed above the cloth's top (white with a light coat); the cloth now follows the back. Its back edge reaches two
  pixels further over the rump, where the donkey's thigh line blinked brown behind it in some walk frames, and its
  back corner curves with the rump, as the Jester's cloth does.
- **Each skin's colour rows start with its biggest part** (2026-11-08): Royal Parade: Saddle cloth, Trim and fringe,
  Emblem, Crown, Jewels. Armored: Body armour, Skirt, Helmet, Plume, Brass. Hellfire: Horns, Lava cracks, Tail, Tail
  tip, Glowing eyes. Only the order changed; every saved colour stays.

## 1.2.0, 2026-11-05

- **Translation fixes** (2026-11-05): Royal Parade's trim and fringe in Brazilian Portuguese and Chinese (texts new in this
  version, as the pack's translations are: not in the player notes).
- **Hellfire's and Armored's outlines follow the Outline row** (2026-11-04): Hellfire's horns and Armored's steel
  lines, with the donkey's own outline (GK2 Donkey API with the Outline row; an older one draws them as before).
- **Hellfire has one row for its eyes** (2026-11-04): Glowing eyes; the donkey's Eyes row is gone, its eyes being
  under the glow in every frame (GK2 Donkey API with it).
- **Each skin's parts have colours of their own** (2026-11-04; the new rows need GK2 Donkey API with tones, and the
  skins read as before on an older one), every part its own. Royal Parade: Saddle cloth, Crown, Trim and fringe,
  Emblem, Jewels. Armored: Helmet, Plume, Body armour, Skirt, Brass. Hellfire: Horns, Glowing eyes, Lava cracks,
  Tail, Tail tip. A part the Harness or Second tone row coloured takes the colour saved for that row once, on the first
  start after the update, so every saved look stays as it was, and is a part of its own from then on; the parts drawn
  as they were start as drawn. Hellfire's dark parts row reads "Legs and mane" (its ears are horns); the outline stays as drawn. Parts that shared one colour have shades of their own now, the same to the eye. The part names are in all
  11 languages.

## 1.1.0, 2026-11-01 (never uploaded: held back over Armored; it ships with 1.2.0)

- **The plugin is named "GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skin Pack"** (2026-10-30; it was "GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skins"): the name BepInEx logs and the framework's Mods
  menu shows is the pack's public name, the one of its Nexus page, its Workshop item and the game's own Mods window
  (which reads it from the zip's `modinfo.json`). The id and the mounts are unchanged.
- **Armored** (2026-10-30, redrawn 2026-11-01/02 before release): a third look for the donkey, plate barding drawn over
  it: an ornate armet (a closed knight's helmet: the skull flush with the head with a gilt comb, a gilt brow, the
  sight with the eye showing through, a beaked visor closed over the muzzle with breaths and a brass rosette where it
  pivots, a roped gilt rim) with a plume that sways in the walk, a chest plate cut round the breast collar, five
  steel lames over the body, a rump plate, a cloth skirt with a dagged hem under the plates and a steel guard of
  rings over the upper half of the tail; ears, tail tuft, legs and the harness stay the donkey's. The steel and the brass are
  always steel and brass, in the shades of the game's own armoured guards (not a player tone); the skirt and the
  plume follow the Second tone, and the harness colour colours the donkey's own harness as on the plain donkey
  (2026-11-02, the user: under the harness colour, the Second tone did nothing on this skin). The first cut (forehead
  spike, neck lames, Second-tone steel, flank lames over padding, a crupper)
  was redrawn after the user's review: on a donkey's head anything that stands out reads as another ear, and a single
  plate over the stretching body slid or seemed to stretch in the walk. The second cut's face plate (a chanfron with
  an eye guard and a brass boss) became the armet after the user saw it in game ("lacklustre", "fill the donkey's face
  more ... like a knight armet type helmet but more ornate"). `donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.armored`; generated by
  `tools/skins/armored/make_armored.py` (the user's references: a war horse in plate, knights' chargers and armets;
  the drawing style from the game's guards).
- **Hellfire and Royal Parade polished** (2026-11-02, the user's review on the preview page and a frame-by-frame
  sweep): Hellfire shows no black block between the horns when the donkey turns to face you (turn 03-06), the tuft
  between the horns sits lower, the far ear no longer shows as a dark slab while turning, and the eyes glow while
  grazing; Royal Parade's crown stays on the head in the turn's three-quarter frame, through the blink, and on the
  lowered head while grazing. Generated by `tools/skins/hellfire/make_hellfire.py` and
  `tools/skins/royal-parade/make_royal.py`.
- **Polished again** (2026-11-03, the user's review on the preview page):
  - **Hellfire:** its lava cracks stay put on the body in every clip, the walk's own cracks (they moved about while
    turning, grazing and standing), and its rump no longer swells while it works its lips.
  - **Royal Parade:** its cloth keeps one shape in every frame. It no longer shifts, stretches or slides in the walk,
    while grazing or while standing, it no longer pulls back as the donkey turns, and the dark mark that rose up the
    collar strap is gone. Its fringe and its point sway very slightly in the walk and while standing ("a little bit
    too staticey", the user, on the first cut of one shape). Drawn once and placed into every frame by
    `tools/skins/stamp.py` (its source from `tools/skins/royal-parade/drawn_once.py`).
  - **Armored:** the rump plate, the rear lames, the skirt's rear and the tail guard sit right in walk 04-06 (a column
    forward before), and a stray bit of skirt in front of the collar in walk 08 is gone.
- The zip holds the DLL alone in its folder, as Saddle Up's zip does; the README.md and CHANGELOG.md that rode
  beside it in 1.0.0 stay in the repo (the user, 2026-10-29).
- **Translatable** (2026-11-01; GK2 Donkey API's translations, its docs/localization.md): the pack ships its English
  as `Languages\en.csv` beside its DLL: its three skins' names (the Skin list, the colours window's title).
  A translator copies it to `de.csv`, `fr.csv`, ... (the game's language ids; GK2 Saddle Up's README says how).
  It shows with a GK2 Donkey API that reads translations (the next release); an older one ignores the folder.
  No code change.
- **French** (2026-11-01): `Languages\fr.csv`, the three skins' names in French when the game is in French.
- **Every language the game has** (2026-11-01): German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish,
  Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean too (`Languages\<id>.csv`).

## 1.0.0, 2026-10-28

- The pack is `GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack`: its DLL, plugin folder (`BepInEx\plugins\GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack\`), source folder
  (`packs\GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack\`) and zip (`GK2-Saddle-Up-Donkey-Skin-Pack-1.0.0.zip`) (the user, 2026-10-28; the pack's
  mount and skin ids, its GUID and its name are unchanged).

- The donkey's two skins as a pack of their own (until 2026-10-27 inside GK2 Saddle Up as `donkey/saddleup.hellfire`
  and `donkey/saddleup.royal-parade`, never released): **Hellfire** (horns, a devil's tail, glowing eyes, lava cracks
  over an ash coat) and **Royal Parade** (a crown and a saddle cloth that follows your harness colour).
- Hellfire's ring of fire is in the pack too, for when skins come with their effects in a later update.
