# GK2 Saddle Up: Donkey Skins

A skins pack for GK2 Saddle Up: four new looks for the donkey.

- **Hellfire:** horns, a devil's tail, glowing eyes and lava cracks over an ash coat. The cracks follow your second
  tone colour.
- **Royal Parade:** a crown and a royal saddle cloth. The cloth follows your harness colour.
- **Armored:** plate barding: a chanfron with an eye hole and a forehead spike, a plume, a chest plate, riveted flank
  lames and a crupper. The steel follows your second tone colour (black iron, bronze, gold), the plume and the padding
  your harness colour.
- **Jester:** a fool's cap with two belled points, a belled collar and a chequered motley caparison with a pointed
  hem. Every part is yours to colour: the cap's two halves and its band, the collar, the caparison's two colours and
  the bells.

- **Needs:** GK2 Donkey API 0.40.0 or newer and GK2 Saddle Up (it offers the skins).
- **Install:** put the folder `GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack` into `BepInEx\plugins\` (the zip has it so; a Workshop item, where there is one, installs it instead):
  `BepInEx\plugins\GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack\GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack.dll`, a plugin with the pack's files inside. To
  update, replace it.
- **In the game:** in the Mounts panel (L), pick the Donkey; its Skin list has Hellfire, Royal Parade, Armored and Jester.
- **Remove:** delete the folder `GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack`. A donkey that wore one of them shows the game's donkey, and
  your choice comes back when the pack does.

The skins: `donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.hellfire`, `donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.royal-parade`,
`donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.armored` and `donkey/saddleup-donkey-skins.jester` (skins for the game's donkey: a folder `donkey\skins\` with no `mount.cfg`),
and Hellfire's effect `saddleup-donkey-skins.hellfire`.
Its files in the repo: `packs/GK2SaddleUpDonkeySkinPack/gk2mounts/`; the pack format: docs/skins.md and
docs/making-a-mount.md.
